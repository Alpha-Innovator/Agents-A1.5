        06 / WHAT IS NEXT

        After reflecting,
        fold the stretch into one record.

        The longer a task runs, the longer the history gets. Next: after reflecting, the model decides for itself whether the stretch it just walked can safely be folded into a stage record that replaces it.

        In development

        After reflecting, deciding whether to fold the stretch into a record

        Reflection judges whether the conclusions hold and where to go next. Immediately after, the model judges one more thing: whether the stretch from the previous reflection up to this one can be safely folded up. If it can, it writes a stage record — what was done, what came of it, which assumptions were ruled out, what state things are in — and that record plus the reflection replace the original stretch. If it cannot, the stretch stays as it is.
